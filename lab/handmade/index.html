1 · THE WORDMARK, KNOCKED OUT OF LINE

Each letter gets its own rotation, size and baseline — re-rolled on every page load, so the wordmark is never laid out twice the same way. The decay you already have keeps running underneath.

"Wild" includes the occasional flipped letter, as on Haocheng's site. Letters are pinned to their widest decay grade first, so none of this can shift while it animates.

2 · MISALIGNMENT, RE-ROLLED EACH VISIT

4 · IMAGES AS OBJECTS

Photographs laid down rather than slotted in: a fraction of a degree off square, with a soft shadow, as though resting on a surface.

Shown on the album covers, but it would apply to work-page galleries and press photos too.
