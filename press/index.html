About Me

Monica is an Australian experimental artist and composer who works primarily with sound. Trained in classical piano, she merges traditional artforms with the most cutting-edge technology, always asking what the future of music is. Her work resists categorisation, melding lighting, embodied movement, participatory practice, circuitry and spatial design with baroque classicism, computer code, glitch and noise. With Malaysian-Chinese and Indonesian heritage, she merges diasporic exploration with Western classical forms. Monica works across screen, installation, dance and performance. Her work has been presented at Sydney Festival, Asia TOPA Festival, Oz Asia, Arts House, Science Gallery Melbourne, Now or Never, Seoul Performing Arts Festival, NIME, Siggraph Asia, ISEA and ArtJog amongst others. She has a doctoral degree in Interactive Composition from the University of Melbourne and is a Research Fellow at the School of Computing.

Music Releases

Most of my work tends to be ephemeral and participatory, created in the moment through structured improvisation, space and audience participation. For over 14 years, I resisted releasing published music, valuing the immediate and imperfect over studio processes. However, I now feel the need to 'put something down'. This year I have released an EP of electronic music based on hacked Buddhist Chanting Machines, and will be releasing an EP of extended piano music using spectral analysis, machine learning and microtonality that is impossible to play on normal pianos.
